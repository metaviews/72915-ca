# 72915

The number is the district: Highway 7, Highway 29 and Highway 15 — the roads that tie Carleton Place and its neighbouring communities together.

72915 is a public feed of information about that district, in three parts: the **district feed** (road work, council decisions, public notices, warnings, community news — short excerpts that link to the original public item), **word of mouth** (what the district is talking about, as seen in local community groups, with a link to each discussion), and a **directory** of people and organizations of the district, curated by us.

## What this is

An open-source intelligence project for the Highway District, in early development by Metaviews. We are building the capacity to collect, organize and publish public information about the area — openly, with a link back to every source we draw on.

What you see here is a working step, not a finished product. The feed updates as our process allows, and the site will grow as the work does. Timestamps are local (Eastern); hashtags are our own growing taxonomy, linking similar items together.

## What this isn't

- **Not a news outlet.** Items are short excerpts from public sources, with a link to the original. The reporting belongs to its publishers.
- **Not a complete picture.** Coverage is still being assembled and gaps are expected. Absence of an item is not evidence that nothing is happening.
- **Not affiliated with any municipality or agency.**

## Corrections

Because every item links to its original source, factual corrections belong with the original publisher first. Feedback about this project itself is welcome — contact details will be published here as the project opens up.
